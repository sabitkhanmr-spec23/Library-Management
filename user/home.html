How borrowing works

  1. Send an application for the book you want.
  2. The librarian approves it and gives you a token.
  3. Show the token at the library desk to collect the book.
  4. The token is valid for 7 days.
  5. You get a notice one day before the return date.
  6. Give the book back to the librarian.
